Build a single-page web app called "India Daily Trend Analyzer" that scans Indian stocks (NSE/BSE) priced ₹200 and above on the DAILY timeframe and gives Buy / Sell / Hold signals.
MARKET SCOPE- Exchanges: NSE (primary, ticker.NS) and BSE (ticker.BO). Currency in ₹.- Screener universes: Nifty 50, Nifty 100, and Nifty Midcap 150 constituent snapshots, plus a starter list. Auto-filter to stocks with last close >= ₹200 (adjustable minimum price input, default 200).- Liquidity filter: skip stocks with 20-day average volume below a user-set minimum (default 100,000 shares).- Handle NSE trading holidays and no-data days, and use split/bonus-adjusted prices.
INPUTS- Ticker search with autocomplete (e.g., RELIANCE, TCS, HDFCBANK)- Lookback selector (3M, 6M, 1Y, 2Y, 5Y)- Trading style: Swing (days to weeks) or Positional (weeks to months)- CSV upload fallback (Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume)
DATA- Fetch daily OHLCV from Yahoo Finance (or a free NSE-compatible source). Cache results, handle rate limits and errors, and fall back to CSV upload.
INDICATORS (daily candles)- EMA 20, 50, 200; SMA 50- RSI (14), MACD (12, 26, 9), Bollinger Bands (20, 2), ATR (14)- ADX (14) for trend strength- Supertrend (10, 3)- Volume vs. 20-day average; flag volume spikes- Support/Resistance from swing highs/lows (20 and 50 days), plus 52-week high/low distance- Candlestick patterns: engulfing, hammer, doji, shooting star, morning/evening star- Relative strength vs. Nifty 50 (stock return vs. index return, 3M)
MARKET REGIME FILTER- Check Nifty 50 against its EMA 50/200. In a bearish regime, downgrade Buy signals by one level and show a warning.
SIGNAL LOGIC- Score each factor -2 to +2: trend (price vs. EMA 50/200, golden/death cross), momentum (RSI, MACD), volatility (Bollinger squeeze/breakout), volume confirmation, ADX/Supertrend, relative strength vs. Nifty- Total score maps to: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Sell, Strong Sell, with a confidence percentage- Show the reason for each factor in plain English
TRADE PLAN OUTPUT- Entry zone, stop-loss (1.5 x ATR or below last swing low), Target 1 and 2, risk:reward ratio- Respect NSE tick size (₹0.05) when rounding prices- Warn if price is near upper/lower circuit limits- Position size calculator: capital (₹), risk % per trade, output whole share quantity- Mode toggle: Delivery (CNC) or Intraday-style exit, for cost estimates
SCREENER- "Scan All" button that runs the logic on the whole filtered universe and shows a sortable table: symbol, price, signal, score, RSI, volume spike, RS vs. Nifty- Filters: Strong Buy only, breakout stocks, near 52-week high, oversold bounce
UI- Candlestick chart with toggleable overlays (EMAs, Bollinger, Supertrend, support/resistance), sub-charts for Volume, RSI, MACD, and buy/sell markers- Signal card (large colored verdict + confidence), indicator table, trade plan card- Watchlist saved in local state with latest signals- Mobile-first, responsive, dark/light mode
BACKTEST- Test the signal logic on the selected history with realistic Indian costs: brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST, stamp duty, and slippage (editable)- Show win rate, net return after costs, max drawdown, trade count, average hold days, and comparison vs. buy-and-hold and vs. Nifty 50
TECH- view + python or golang, Lightweight Charts (TradingView), modular code (data, indicators, signals, screener, backtest, UI), unit tests for indicator calculations
COMPLIANCE- Show the disclaimer: "For educational purposes only. Not investment advice. Not SEBI-registered advice. Markets involve risk."
DELIVERABLES- Full working code, setup instructions, and a short explanation of how signal scoring works.
